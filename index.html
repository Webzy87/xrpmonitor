📖 MARKET EXPLAINER ▼
Buy Pressure
How much volume is coming in on the buy side. High = more people actively buying.
Cumulative Delta
Running total of buy volume minus sell volume.
  • Positive = buyers have done more volume
  • Negative = sellers have done more
  • Rising = buying momentum increasing
Order Book Depth
All waiting buy and sell orders. Thick bid side = strong support below price. Thick ask side = resistance above.
Absorption
When one side absorbs the other's pressure without price moving much. Buyers absorbing sells = bullish sign.
Large Trades / Whales
Orders over 100K XRP. These are institutions or large holders. Their direction often matters more than retail flow.
Iceberg Orders
A large order broken into many smaller ones to hide its true size. Repeated prints of similar XRP amount = likely one hidden order being worked.
VWAP
Volume-Weighted Average Price for the session. Price above VWAP = bullish. Below = bearish. Think of it as today's fair value.
Jon Score
A 0–100 score combining all signals.
  • 0 = Extreme selling pressure
  • 50 = Balanced
  • 100 = Extreme buying pressure
