📖 MARKET EXPLAINER ▼

Buy Pressure — how much volume is coming in on the buy side. High buy pressure means more people are actively buying.

Delta — the difference between buy volume and sell volume. Positive = buyers in control. Negative = sellers.

Order Book Depth — all the buy and sell orders waiting. A thick bid side means strong support below price.

Absorption — when one side absorbs the other's pressure without price moving much. Buyers absorbing sells = bullish.

Large Trades — orders over 100k XRP. These are institutions or large holders. Their direction often matters more than retail.

Iceberg — when a large order is broken into many smaller ones to hide its size. Repeated prints of similar size = one big hidden order.
